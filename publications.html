Redefining the tumor digital twin through cross-domain manifold mapping.

Yuanning Hu, Wei Zong, Juncheng Wang, Manqi Cai, Jian Zou*. The Innovation Oncology, 1, 100012.

This commentary discusses how cross-domain manifold mapping can reshape the concept of a tumor digital twin by aligning preclinical models with human tumors in a shared representation space. The perspective emphasizes statistical congruence across biological systems and the potential of model–patient matching for precision oncology.
