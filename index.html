Statistical learning and multi-omics integration for cancer prognosis.

I am a postgraduate from the Department of Statistics, School of Public Health, Chongqing Medical University. My work focuses on clinically grounded statistical and machine-learning methods for high-dimensional biomedical data, with a particular interest in breast cancer recurrence and interpretable multi-omics modeling.

About

I apply quantitative methods that connect clinical questions with high-dimensional molecular data. My current interests include early recurrence prediction in HR+/HER2− breast cancer, fair comparison of single-omics and multi-omics models, and biologically interpretable network analysis.
