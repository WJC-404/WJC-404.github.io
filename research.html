Research

From molecular data
to clinical insight.

My work examines when complex molecular measurements add clinically meaningful information—and when a simpler, stronger data layer may already capture most of the predictive signal.

Line 01

Breast cancer recurrence

Developing prognostic models for early recurrence in HR+/HER2− breast cancer by combining clinical variables with transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic data. The emphasis is on realistic evaluation, leakage control, and stable performance under limited sample size.

Line 02

Multi-omics integration

Comparing supervised latent-variable, stacking, survival-integration, and clinically anchored models under a common evaluation framework. A central question is whether full integration provides meaningful incremental value beyond the strongest single molecular layer.

Line 03

Interpretable biomedical modeling

Linking predictive performance to biological interpretation through differential analysis, pathway enrichment, cross-omics comparison, and patient-specific sample networks. The goal is to identify signals that are not only predictive, but also mechanistically coherent and clinically understandable.
